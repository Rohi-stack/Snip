About DashURL

We build modern link management tools for the modern web.

Why we exist

The internet is built on links. But for too long, link management has been treated as a utility rather than a strategic asset. Most URL shorteners are clunky, outdated, or overly expensive.

We built DashURL to provide a premium, lightning-fast platform that respects both your time and your data.

What we do

DashURL isn't just about making links smaller. It's about making them smarter. Our platform provides:

  • Custom Aliases: Brand your links so they are memorable and trustworthy.
  • Premium QR Codes: Generate high-quality SVG and PNG QR codes instantly.
  • Real-time Analytics: Track where your audience is coming from, what devices they use, and which campaigns perform best.

Our Design Philosophy

We believe enterprise-grade software doesn't have to look like a spreadsheet. We designed DashURL with a dark, modern aesthetic inspired by the tools developers and designers actually enjoy using every day.
