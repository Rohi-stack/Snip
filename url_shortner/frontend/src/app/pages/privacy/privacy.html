Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account with Snip, we collect your email address and basic profile information. If you use Google Authentication, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.

2. Link Analytics

To provide our core service, we collect analytics data when users click on your shortened links. This data includes:

  • IP Addresses (used only to determine rough geographic location like Country/City)
  • Browser and OS user agents
  • Referrer URLs (where the click came from)
  • Timestamps of the clicks

We do not use this data to personally identify the end-users clicking the links. This data is surfaced to you (the link creator) in your dashboard.

3. Cookies and Sessions

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your authenticated session (JWT tokens), remember your preferences, and protect against security threats. You can disable cookies in your browser, but this will prevent you from logging into the dashboard.

4. Payment Processing

If you upgrade to a paid plan, your payment information is processed directly by Razorpay. Snip does not store or process your full credit card details. We only store a reference to your subscription status and billing cycle.
